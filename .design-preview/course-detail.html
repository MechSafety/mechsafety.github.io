Overview

This course introduces the formulation and implementation of computational methods for solving mechanics problems. Emphasis is placed on the connection between physical assumptions, mathematical models, numerical algorithms, and engineering interpretation.

What students learn

  • Translate mechanics problems into computational models.
  • Understand core discretization and solution strategies.
  • Assess numerical accuracy, convergence, and stability.
  • Interpret simulation results with appropriate engineering judgment.

Main topics

Governing equations, weak forms, discretization, nonlinear solution methods, time integration, verification, validation, and selected applications.
